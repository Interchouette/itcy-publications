The gap between AI features and revenue is often a visibility problem, not a product one. Companies can build sophisticated models that drive genuine value yet fail to monetize because they lack granular telemetry on how customers actually engage with specific capabilities. Without this data, you are pricing assumptions rather than evidence, creating a significant risk for hybrid pricing models that rely on usage tiers to prevent churn and optimize packaging. To close the loop, organizations must shift from guessing customer willingness to pay to measuring it against real-world engagement metrics that directly correlate to perceived value.

This insight transforms the challenge of AI monetization into a straightforward engineering discipline rooted in precise data tracking. By implementing robust telemetry, product teams can distinguish between features that deliver tangible benefits and those that are merely experimental fluff, allowing them to align their billing strategies with actual consumption patterns. When pricing is grounded in these insights, customers feel they are paying a fair price for specific outcomes rather than an ambiguous subscription fee. The result is a sustainable revenue model where visibility drives adoption, evidence-based decisions replace guesswork, and the potential of Generative AI investments finally translates into measurable business growth. 🦀 🦉
