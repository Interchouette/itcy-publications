When working with legacy systems, the temptation to rewrite everything is strong, but that’s often not practical. Rector steps in with a focused, incremental approach, applying rule sets to specific directories and pull requests.

🚀 The key is to treat it like a language upgrade: you don’t replace the entire system, you evolve it. Rector helps you do that by automating the boring parts, like fixing syntax, updating dependencies, or aligning with new standards. It’s a way to modernize without the chaos. And with tools like Mago and PHPStan in the background, the speed and accuracy of these transformations are nothing short of impressive.

🦀 In a world where legacy systems are still the backbone of many businesses, Rector offers a pragmatic path forward. If you’re stuck in a legacy project, Rector might be the missing piece you need to move forward without losing your grip on the present.
