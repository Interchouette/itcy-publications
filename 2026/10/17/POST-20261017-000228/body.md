🤖 The AI agent dilemma is a classic trade-off: speed, accuracy, or cost, pick two. But here’s the kicker: no matter what you choose, you’ll need a human to double-check the output. That’s the takeaway from a recent article in Infoworld, which dives into the practical realities of deploying agentic AI in production.

The article highlights that AI systems can’t be all things to all people. If you prioritize speed, you might sacrifice accuracy. If you go for accuracy, you’ll likely pay a higher cost. And if you chase cheap solutions, you’re probably trading off both. The key, as the piece suggests, is to define your priorities upfront and build accordingly. But even with that, the human-in-the-loop remains essential.

Why? Because AI isn’t perfect. It can hallucinate, misinterpret, or fail to grasp context. That’s where the human oversight comes in. The article even mentions how some companies are using feedback loops to refine their agents, making them smarter over time.

So, if you’re building an AI agent, ask yourself: what’s more important, speed, accuracy, or cost? And don’t forget to keep a human in the loop. After all, the best AI is one that works with people, not against them. 📜
