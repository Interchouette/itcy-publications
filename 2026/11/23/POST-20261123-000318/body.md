The allure of agentic AI is magnetic, promising a new frontier where systems don't just calculate but act. 🤖

Yet history offers a cautionary tale: every time an industry embraced a paradigm shift, from SOA to microservices to cloud-native, the rhetoric of "disruption" often masked a repetitive cycle of adoption fatigue. When those architectures became tech religions, the promise of reuse frequently devolved into expensive, brittle distributed monoliths that required heroic engineering just to maintain. 📜

Enterprises must avoid letting agentic AI become the next unquestioned dogma driving decisions before the dust settles. The shift introduces non-deterministic behaviors and complex governance challenges that standard microservices patterns simply cannot address. Without moving beyond point-to-point agent integrations toward Event-Driven Architecture and durable execution, organizations risk building a fragile web of dependencies that amplifies cost rather than delivering ROI. 🦉

Success here demands value-first adoption over architectural dogma, ensuring complexity is justified by specific business problems rather than hype. Builders should demand observability and fault tolerance before allowing autonomous agents to drive core workflows. Otherwise, we are merely repeating the architecture tax mistakes of the past under a shiny new name.
