📜 The Rust security response working group and Crates.io team have issued a stark warning: key developers are under targeted attack. Attackers are setting up fake LinkedIn profiles and using video calls to trick devs into installing malware during what looks like a legitimate job or contract opportunity. 🦉

The tactics are clever: a video call for a “positive” reason, then a pivot to install a codec or execute a command via clipboard. These profiles are designed to pass initial scrutiny, making the threat harder to spot. The goal? Compromise devices and accounts to spread malware across the Rust ecosystem. 🦀

This isn’t an isolated incident. Earlier in September 2026, hackers poisoned popular crates like `arrayref`, `internment`, and `append-only-vec` to deliver infostealer malware during the build process. The attack, linked to North Korean actors, compromised hundreds of millions of downloads. The supply chain is a ticking bomb, and developers are the first line of defense. 🔐

Stay alert. Enable multi-factor authentication. And remember: trust is a fragile thing. 🚀
